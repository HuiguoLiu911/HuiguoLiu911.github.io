What does off-policy travel cost?

Analytics for Good Hackathon · Process analytics on enterprise travel data in a 24-hour sprint

The question

The Analytics for Good Hackathon at the Carlson School of Management gave teams 24 hours with enterprise travel and process-mining data and a sustainability theme. Our question: does travel booked outside company policy cost more than money, specifically in carbon?

Data

Enterprise travel bookings and the process-mining event data behind them, supplied by the hackathon. No individual traveler data appears here.

Method

Working in SQL, Tableau and Celonis, we separated out-of-policy trips from in-policy trips and compared their carbon emissions and cost. I worked on the SQL analysis and the Tableau views.

Results

  • Out-of-policy travel was associated with 46.3% higher carbon emissions than in-policy travel, with minimal difference in cost.
  • The entry placed Top 3 among finalists.

What it changed

The finding framed policy compliance as a sustainability lever rather than only a cost control, and the Tableau views carried the comparison in the team presentation.

Limitations and next steps

This is an association from a hackathon dataset; it does not separate trip purpose, route or traveler role from the policy effect. A next step is to control for route and distance before claiming a causal effect.

Code & materials

This work was delivered inside a client engagement; no public code is available. Happy to walk through the approach on a call.
